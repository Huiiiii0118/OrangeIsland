## 改进

### 展开面板

- 通知、音乐、系统监控、剪贴板与格式转换五个页面尺寸统一，切换页面时面板大小保持不变。
- 快捷尺寸提供紧凑、标准、宽敞三档；调整任一页尺寸即同步到所有页面。
- 音乐页与系统监控页底部留白收紧，内容更贴底，不再与右下角的「展开 / 收起」按钮挤在一起。

### 音乐

- 音量条加宽近一倍，更好点按和拖动；按住拖动时，填充、百分比与实际音量实时同步。

### 系统监控

- 进程列表与帧率、电源、磁盘及 CPU / 内存 / 网络读数一屏看全，切换页面后依然完整保留。

### 通知

- 左栏没有选中内容时，居中提示「点击右侧通知查看内容」。

### 关于

- 「关于」页新增「第三方声明」入口，声明随程序内置，可直接查看和复制。

## Improvements

### Expanded panel

- All five pages — notifications, music, system monitor, clipboard and conversion — now share one size, so the panel keeps the same shape when you switch pages.
- Three quick sizes are available: compact, standard and spacious; resizing any page applies to all.
- The music and system monitor pages sit closer to the bottom, leaving less empty space and keeping clear of the "expand / collapse" buttons in the corner.

### Music

- The volume bar is nearly twice as wide and easier to grab; dragging it updates the fill, the percentage and the actual volume in real time.

### System monitor

- The process list sits alongside frame rate, power, disk and the CPU / memory / network readings, all visible at once and kept intact when switching pages.

### Notifications

- When nothing is selected, the left pane shows a centered hint to pick a notice on the right.

### About

- The About page gains a "Third-party notices" entry. The notices are built into the app and can be read and copied on the spot.
