## 改进

### 软件更新

- 下载更新期间，主面板与设置窗口保持可用，不再被更新窗口挡住操作。
- 更新提示新增「后台下载并安装」：点击后关闭提示窗口，在后台静默下载，下载完成后自动安装并重启，全程可继续使用软件。
- 后台更新时，「关于」页会显示更新进度；更新结束或取消后自动恢复原有文字与按钮。
- 安装完成后，自动清理旧版本安装文件与下载缓存，不再占用磁盘空间。

### 通知

- 修复钉钉消息被误识别为微信的问题；此前误判产生的重复通知也会自动清理。
- 点开邮件通知后，胶囊上的未读提醒会立即取消。

### 邮件

- 修复部分邮件正文显示为乱码的问题，正确识别编码与正文内容。
- 优化营销邮件的正文提取，优先展示可读内容。

## Improvements

### App updates

- The main panel and Settings stay usable while an update downloads; the update window no longer blocks interaction.
- The update prompt gains "Download in background": it closes the prompt, downloads quietly, then installs and restarts automatically when ready — the app stays usable throughout.
- While a background update runs, the About page shows its progress and returns to its normal labels and buttons once the update finishes or is cancelled.
- After installation, old installation files and the download cache are cleaned up automatically, freeing disk space.

### Notifications

- Fixed DingTalk messages being misidentified as WeChat; duplicate notices caused by the earlier misidentification are cleaned up as well.
- Opening a mail notification now clears the unread badge on the capsule right away.

### Mail

- Fixed garbled text in some mail bodies by detecting the encoding and picking the readable content correctly.
- Improved body selection for promotional mail, preferring the readable version.
