## 改进

### 软件更新

- 更新信息在独立的「软件更新」窗口中展示：版本、体积与更新说明一目了然，可在窗口内直接「下载更新」「检查更新」或「跳过此版本」。
- 新增「后台更新」按钮：点击后关闭更新窗口，下载在后台继续，主面板与设置窗口保持可用；下载完成后仍会先询问是否安装，不会自动安装。
- 下载完成后，应用内自动弹出安装确认浮层并跳到「关于」页，由你选择「稍后」或「安装并重启」。浮层跟随应用的主题、窗口尺寸与 DPI，不会遗留独立弹窗。
- 点击「稍后」会立即停止下载并关闭更新窗口，软件照常使用，本次运行不再提示；下次启动软件时自动重新弹出更新信息。
- 修复「稍后」与下载完成恰好同时发生时，安装确认浮层可能残留的问题。
- 修复取消下载后紧接着重新开始下载时，新任务可能被中断的问题。

## Improvements

### App updates

- Update details appear in a dedicated "Software update" window: version, size and release notes at a glance, with "Download update", "Check for updates" and "Skip this version" right there.
- A new "Update in background" button closes the update window while the download continues. The main panel and Settings stay usable, and you are still asked to confirm before anything is installed — nothing installs automatically.
- When a download finishes, an in-app confirmation appears over the About page: choose "Later" or "Install and restart". The overlay follows the app theme, window size and DPI, and leaves no stray dialog behind.
- "Later" stops the download immediately, closes the window, keeps the app fully usable and stays quiet for this run. The update is offered again automatically the next time the app starts.
- Fixed a confirmation overlay that could linger when "Later" was pressed at the exact moment a download finished.
- Fixed a new download being interrupted when started right after a cancelled one.
